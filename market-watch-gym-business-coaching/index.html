This week's moves
1

NPE moved 48 new ads to one diagnostic page on Aug 19

Sean Greeley launched 48 ads on Aug 19 and every one of the 60 I captured points at the same page: 'Diagnose what's actually capping your growth, and get your personalized 90-day plan.' The copy refuses the lead problem outright: 'Usually it's pricing. Retention. Or the order you're doing things in.' It is the biggest single-page bet in your lane, and at 13 days old nothing is proven yet. Your 60 captured ads split across six pages, 30 of them straight to a consultation. The test: one short what's-capping-your-gym assessment page, fed by three of your current statics, two weeks, judged on cost per booked consultation against the direct-to-call ads. See the ad →

2

The lane's most proven creative is a 131-day audit story

Bob Thompson has paid for the same opener since Apr 23: 'I audited a failing gym in Utah last week. They had great equipment and terrible offers. Here is the exact offer reframe that brought 3 new clients in 24 hours.' 57 of his 60 captured ads are past 30 days, all to one page promising 20 to 30 new clients a month. Your case-study ads tell the outcome; his tell the diagnosis, and the diagnosis survived four months. The test: one to-camera story from a real mentorship call, what was wrong and what changed, to your free tools page, judged on cost per lead against the millionaire-count ads. See it →

3

The GLP-1 angle has run 60 days and no coach owns it

Gymtopia Automations & Advertising, an agency, has run 'You do NOT need to sell GLP-1s. But you DO need to understand how they're changing your gym' since Jul 3, 7 of its 15 ads past 30 days, all to a page headed 'Find your next 10 clients from the same place you're losing them to.' Every coach in your lane is still selling systems and freedom. Nobody is selling the answer to the market shift every owner is feeling this year. The test: one static naming the shift and one thing a gym does about it, to your free tools page, judged on cost per lead against your current best. See it →

4

A launch-date calculator has run 109 days one lane over

Lori Kennedy, who coaches practitioners taking a practice online, has run a 31-second video since May 15: 'Add the date you want to be working with clients and let my calculator tell you when you need to start.' 29 of her 60 captured ads are past 30 days. It is a tool ad, not a promise ad, and nobody in the gym-owner lane runs one. Your free tools page has 20 tools behind it and no ad that shows a single one working. The test: one video of one calculator from your toolkit doing its job, to the free tools page, judged on cost per lead against the toolkit statics. See it →
