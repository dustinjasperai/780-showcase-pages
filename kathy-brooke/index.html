The numbers

The figures below use a conservative benchmark for UK leadership and communication coaching. Swap in your own number and it only moves one way.

At $1,500 a month in ad spend, registrations tend to land around $10 to $15, so roughly 120 people a month opt in to the live session.

About 30% show up live, so around 36 attendees. Of those, 10 to 20% book a call, call it 5 calls a month. Close two of them into a benchmark $2,500 engagement and that is $5,000 a month from $1,500 of spend, before a single person touches your existing platform.
