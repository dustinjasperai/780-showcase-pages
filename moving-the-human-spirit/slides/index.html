Foundations

The Window of Tolerance, in 90 seconds

Every client sits somewhere between three states.

  • Hyperarousal above the window: anxiety, overwhelm, fight or flight
  • The window: present, resourced, able to think and feel at the same time
  • Hypoarousal below the window: numb, shut down, disconnected
