The numbers, conservatively

I do not know your exact Board Readiness Program fee, so the maths uses a deliberately conservative $2,200 benchmark for a European board-readiness cohort. Peer executive board programs run far higher (MIT Sloan lists $16,000), so treat this as a floor.

At $1,500 a month in ad spend, a professional audience like yours registers at roughly $10 to $15 each, so about 100 to 150 registrations a month. If 30% attend the live masterclass and a conservative 3% of registrants go on to apply and enrol, that is three to five new Board Readiness places a month. Even at the $2,200 floor, that is $6,600 to $11,000 a month in new program revenue from $1,500 of spend, before the memberships the same funnel brings in.

Planning estimates, not promises. Real numbers depend on your fee and audience.
