The numbers

The masterclass is its own funnel: paid ads send people to a registration page, they watch you live, and a share of them move to a connection call. At around $1,500 a month in ad spend, registrations in this space tend to run $10 to $15, so roughly 100 to 150 people register each month. If about 30% attend live, that is 30 to 45 people hearing your thinking in full, every month, with a list that comes back for every session after.

I don't know your pricing, so this uses a conservative benchmark of around $1,200 for a coaching engagement. Even if only a couple of attendees a month begin coaching, the funnel more than pays for its own ad spend, and the reach compounds from there.
