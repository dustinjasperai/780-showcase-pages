What the numbers look like

At $1,500 a month in ad spend, and a conservative benchmark of roughly $6 a registration for a broad consumer hobby audience like this one, you'd see in the region of 250 registrations a month. Blues harmonica is cheap to reach, so I think that benchmark is on the safe side.

Typically about 30% show up live, so call it 75 people in the room each session. Even if only 3% of registrants start your $39 a month membership, that's roughly 7 or 8 new members a month, about $300 in new recurring revenue added every month, and because it's a membership it compounds as those members stay. That is the floor, running alongside everything you already do.
