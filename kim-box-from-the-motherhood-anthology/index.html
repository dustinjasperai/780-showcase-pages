Why six more, when you already have 6 running

Right now The Motherhood Anthology has 6 ads running, so volume isn't the gap.

Meta's own guidance is that performance comes from variety: distinct concepts, formats and messages, not more of one. Their published best practice is up to ten creatives per ad set, keeping the top five, tested at least four days each.

Meta asks you to vary three things: concept, the message and tone. format, static against video, feed against Reels. audience, different messages for different needs. Their own reported figures for doing that are 32% more efficient direct-response outcomes and 9% incremental reach, and campaigns running both static and video saw conversion lift at a 17% higher rate than static alone. Those are Meta's numbers, published to advertisers.
