What $1,500 a month could do

At $1,500 a month in ads, at a rough $10 to $15 per registration (a cautious estimate for cold traffic, since your own numbers will land differently), you are looking at somewhere around 100 to 150 registrations a month.

If 30% show up to the live masterclass and 10 to 20% of those book a call, that is roughly 3 to 9 new discovery calls a month into your one-on-one coaching, from people who had never heard of you.

Those numbers are deliberately conservative, and they are reach, not a revenue promise. The point is a predictable, always-on top of funnel instead of hoping the right person finds you.
