The numbers

Here is the math, anchored to your real prices. At about $1,500 a month in ad spend, small-town founder registrations tend to run around $10 to $15 each, so call it 100 to 150 people a month into the live room. Roughly 30 percent show up live, and a handful of those book. One FOUND client at $4,500 more than covers the whole month. Two, and the funnel pays for itself several times over, on top of the strategy sessions some will book on the way in. I think that is a conservative read, and the point is it runs every month, not a few times a year the way a speaking season does.
