Stage 0 · Foundations

The leak: no clear offer

Legal setup, your gear, and a birth photography offer a parent instantly understands. Most people rush past this and wonder why nobody books.

Stuck here if: people love your work but never ask the price.

Stage 1 · First paid clients

The leak: waiting to be found

You are ready, but you are waiting for referrals and the feed to bring people to you. Your first paid clients come from being findable and reaching your own town on purpose.

Stuck here if: you have booked a few, but never on demand.

The leak: feast or famine

Some months are full, some are empty, and you cannot predict which. Consistency comes from a repeatable content system, not from posting when you remember to.

Stuck here if: your income swings hard month to month.

The system, Part Two

Book birth clients through owned content

Owned content marketing means being found by the parents in your area who are already searching, and turning your own town into clients, so your bookings do not depend on the feed.

Three pillars make it repeatable.
