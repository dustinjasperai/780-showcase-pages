The numbers

The masterclass is the centerpiece of a second funnel: always-on ads register creators for the live session, the session does the teaching, and the right people move on to your discovery call. Here is what a starter budget buys each month, using the show rates typical for a funnel like this.

  • $1,500 a month in a starter ad budget, running continuously
  • Roughly 100 to 150 registrations a month at a typical cost per registration for this audience
  • About 30% show up live, so 30 to 45 creators watch you teach every month
  • Of those, 10 to 20% book a call, feeding your existing discovery-call step

I have kept revenue out of this on purpose. I do not know your enrollment price, so putting a dollar figure on the outcome would be a guess, and guesses are not much use to you. On the call we would run it against your real Podcast With Purpose numbers. What the budget line shows is the flow of new people it puts in front of you, not a promised return.
