The numbers

The math below uses a deliberately conservative benchmark for one-on-one ministry coaching, and it still works. At about $1,500 a month in ad spend, registrations for an audience like yours tend to run around $12, so roughly 125 people a month opt in to the live masterclass. Near 30% show up, so you are teaching to around 35 warm people every session. If just two or three of them move into coaching at a conservative $2,000, that is $4,000 to $6,000 a month in new coaching on $1,500 of spend, with the podcast and book still feeding the top of it.

The $2,000 figure is a flagged industry benchmark, not your price. Plug in your real number and the math moves with it.
