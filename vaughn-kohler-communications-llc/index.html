How the numbers work

I don't know your exact coaching fee, so the math below uses a conservative $5,000 benchmark for a high-ticket engagement; your real number is very likely higher. At $1,500 a month in ad spend, registrations for a masterclass like this tend to run $9 to $15, so roughly 100 to 165 practice owners register each month. Around 30% show up live, and of those we usually see 10 to 20% book a call. Even at the low end that is a handful of qualified calls a month you weren't getting, from people who sat through an hour of your thinking before they ever spoke to you. Close two at the benchmark and the channel has paid for itself several times over. These are illustrative benchmarks, not a promise; your real numbers depend on your offer and your close rate.
